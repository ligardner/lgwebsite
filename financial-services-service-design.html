Supply-chain finance service design.

After a merger, one company brought the operational expertise and the other the digital platform. I connected them: modelling the operation, identifying the bottlenecks and leading the work to a development-ready MVP.

What I did, in four moves

  1. 01 ChallengeSituation

    Two merged companies had the capabilities, but not yet a shared service.

    The teams had never worked together, and the brief covered three modules under a hard platform timeline. I reframed it around a more fundamental question.

    How could the new platform improve a critical service and show the first value of the merger?

  2. 02 UnderstandTask

    Model the operation and find where the service was breaking down.

    I led the modelling of the end-to-end process across buyers, suppliers and operations.

    Dependencies and service pain made visible.

  3. 03 AlignAction

    Introduce customer-centric shared ways of working.

    I used co-design workshops, shared service maps and evidence to bring operations, product, SMEs and engineering around the same customer outcomes.

    A customer-centred way of working across the newly combined organisation.

  4. 04 DeliverResult

    Turn service insight into a tangible MVP ready for development.

    • Validated workflow
    • Development-ready design
    • Clear Now / Next / Later roadmap for scale

    3 modules → 1 priority MVP, development-ready in 8 weeks.
